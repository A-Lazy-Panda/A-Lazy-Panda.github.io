Assumptions for Solow growth f(K,L)f(K,L)f(K,L):

  • I Population growth is exogenously determined and independent of income
  • II Depreciation is a constant proportion of the capital stock (also exogenous)
  • III Savings are a constant proportion of national income (exogenous)
  • IV Savings equal investment. Closed economy with no government, so Y=C+IY = C + IY=C+I
