Thesis for a long recommendation

This is the fastest-growing company in a stable and consolidating sector where size is the only competitive advantage, with an estimated European market share of 1.7% [E, original summary].

This is a typical value stock. It is a family-run, high-growth company in a mature and boring sector. It has already shown that it can expand successfully and still has plenty of room to grow in Poland and beyond. The intended holding period was two to three years [E, original summary].

Operations and clients

Auto Partner is an aftermarket parts distributor for vehicles. The company is a logistics platform and distributes the purchased spare parts to the end customers.

In Poland, 62% of its customers are workshops, 30% are specialised shops and the remaining 8% are non-specialised shops [U, original summary figures]. Exports go to shops, with higher margins. Online sales are a growth prospect.

Revenue is anti-cyclical. In a recession, new vehicle sales fall. This causes an increase in the age of the fleet, resulting in more maintenance needs.

Positive trends

The average age of cars is increasing. The summary cited 12.3 years in Europe from ACEA and 14.2 years in Poland from Statista [U, sources as cited in the original summary].

The fleet of vehicles in circulation is growing slowly. From 2016 to 2022, it grew at 1.1% per year [U, Eurostat as cited in the original summary].

The market is still fragmented and undergoing consolidation. Scale is the key in this industry; consolidation is inevitable.

Growth

Growth has been impressive and purely organic, at a CAGR of 26.4% since the IPO [U, original summary; the end date is not specified]. Auto Partner’s strategy is to continue increasing the scale of operations, diversify products, increase profitability and expand sales in foreign markets.

Growth in Poland has been very good, with a CAGR of 16.2%, but international growth has been even better, with a CAGR of 35.9% [U, original summary; periods not specified]. This is very interesting because in 2023 international sales were 50% of the total [U, original summary]. Together with the forces of consolidation, this will allow the company to continue to maintain a high growth rate.
