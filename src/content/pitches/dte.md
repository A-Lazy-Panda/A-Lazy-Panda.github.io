*Cambridge Student Investment Fund pitch, March 2025 — team: Shivansh Pandey, Jade Chen, Josh Tan, Rafael Karapetian, Yvonne Dai. Target and upside are the team's estimates at the time of writing.*

## Thesis

Long Deutsche Telekom to a **€50.11 target price, ~38% upside [E]**. The core of the case is that DTE's controlling position in its US wireless business is worth more than the consolidated European multiple implies, while the European operations quietly de-lever and improve. The market prices DTE as a slow European incumbent; the sum of the parts says otherwise.

## The debate

The equity question is whether you are paying a European telco multiple for an asset whose largest value driver behaves like a US growth compounder. When the controlled US stake is marked at its own market value and the stub is examined on its own cash flows, the implied valuation of the rest of the group looks conservative.

## What would break it

The thesis leans on the US franchise sustaining subscriber and ARPU momentum and on continued deleveraging at the group. It breaks if US competitive intensity re-rates the wireless business down, or if capital allocation (spectrum, M&A) consumes the free cash flow that the deleveraging case depends on.
