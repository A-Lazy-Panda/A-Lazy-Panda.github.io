Long Duolingo to a **'25 target of $176.13, about 85% upside and a 21% IRR [E]**. The core claim is that subscription penetration is being underrated because the market misunderstands the core cohort, and is heading for an inflection rather than a plateau.

The revenue splits roughly into subscription at **72% [R]** (premium, where the penetration argument lives), advertising at **15% [R]** (in-app ads to free users, with room for ad-per-user to rise as the product improves), the Duolingo English Test at **10% [R]** (accepted by 18 of the top-20 US undergraduate programmes, and already about 10% of the market within two years of launch), and other at **3% [R]**.

It breaks if paid conversion settles at today's cohort economics, or if the English Test stops taking share from the incumbents.
