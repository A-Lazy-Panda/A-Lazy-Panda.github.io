## Thesis

Long Duolingo to a **'25 price target of $176.13 (~85% upside, ~21% IRR) [E]**. The core claim: subscription penetration growth is *understated* because the market misunderstands DUOL's customer cohort, and is heading for a major inflection.

## Segment view (as pitched)

- **Subscription — ~72% of revenue [R].** Premium (Duolingo Plus/Super). The penetration-inflection argument lives here.
- **Advertising — ~15% [R].** In-app ads to free users; an improving UX and data moat drive ad$/user.
- **English Test — ~10% [R].** The Duolingo English Test, accepted by 18 of the top-20 US undergraduate programmes, with ~10% share taken from incumbents inside two years — evidence of a superior product.
- **Other — ~3% [R].**

## What would break it

The bull case rests on subscription conversion inflecting rather than maturing, and on the English Test's share gains continuing against entrenched incumbents. It breaks if paid conversion plateaus at current cohort economics.
